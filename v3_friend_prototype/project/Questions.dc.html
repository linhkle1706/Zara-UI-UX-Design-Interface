USER TESTING · AFTER THE PROTOTYPE

Feedback questions: is the new version really better?

Testers answer while they use the app: each numbered function has its questions next to it. The final choice comes at the end. All answers are added together into one result for each function.

Open prototype + feedback →
1Try the screenTap the NEW phone. The OLD phone shows the same step. 13 screens, 17 numbered functions.
2Rate the OLD oneUnder each number: “How good is the OLD one?” 1–5. Low scores = worst old functions.
3Rate the NEW one“How good is the NEW one?” 1–5. High scores = best new functions.
4Old, same or new?“Which do you prefer?” Old · Same · New. Then go to the next screen.
5Your final choice (at the end)How often you shop on a phone · the ONE new function you like most · the ONE old function to keep · overall score 1–5 · what we changed too much · what still needs work.

How we put the answers together Each function gets one result. The rules are checked from top to bottom.
Over-changed40%+ prefer the old way, OR 25%+ want to keep it, OR the old one scores higher than the new one.
Not upgraded well25%+ give the new one 1 or 2, OR 50%+ see no difference, OR the new score is below 3.5.
Successful upgrade60%+ prefer the new way, the new score is 4+, AND fewer than 20% give it 1 or 2.
MixedEverything else: test again with more people.
Is the new version good overall? Yes, if most functions are “Successful upgrade” AND the average “new is better” score is 4 or more out of 5. Every “Over-changed” function is a candidate to bring back the old way. Every “Not upgraded well” function goes back to redesign.
The form saves answers for people who have edit access. Other testers get an answer code to send to the team. The team adds the codes in Results. Results tab = live charts + CSV
