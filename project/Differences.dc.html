What changed and why

Six main problems in the current app, and how the new design fixes each one.

Section
Current app
Redesign
Nielsen heuristic
Home & menu
A small menu icon with no label. Users must guess where things are.
A bottom tab bar with 5 labelled tabs. Category chips on the home page.
#6 Recognition, not recall
Search & list
Search is hidden inside the menu. No suggestions. Names and prices are tiny.
Search is one tap away, with popular words. Clear prices, a price filter and sorting.
#7 Flexibility & efficiency
Product page
Sizes appear only after tapping “Add”, in a pop-up. No size guide.
Sizes are visible at once, with a size guide. The button stays off until a size is chosen.
#5 Error prevention
Bag & checkout
A login wall first. “Guest” is a tiny link. 4 separate screens. Shipping cost comes late.
Guest checkout by default. One page with 3 clear steps and a full price summary.
#1 Visibility of system status
Account & help
Help is a long page of small text. No chat or call button.
Chat and Call buttons at the top. Short answers that open on tap.
#10 Help & documentation
Readability
8–11 px text, all capitals, light grey. Hard to read. Small tap areas.
13–17 px text, normal case, strong contrast. Tap areas of 44 px or more.
